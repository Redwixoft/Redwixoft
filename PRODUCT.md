## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people who come across one of Redwixoft's apps or games and want to know more — players and parents who arrive from a store listing, a link or a search, and want to see what the product is, how it works and where to get it.

Secondary: companies looking for a small studio to build .NET or AI-powered software, who use the products as evidence that Redwixoft ships and maintains real things.

App-store reviewers and players also land on the Abecedo privacy and account-deletion pages, which Google Play links to.

## Product Purpose

The home of Redwixoft s. r. o., a small independent software studio in Bratislava. The site presents the studio's own products first and its client services second. Success is a visitor understanding what each product is and reaching it (store, product site), and a prospective client finding enough proof and a direct way to email.

## Positioning

A studio that builds and runs its own consumer products, not only client work. Its claim to clients rests on those shipped products.

## Product Principles

1. Products lead; services follow from them.
2. Every product page shows what the thing actually is and how to get it.
3. Each product keeps its own identity; the studio frame stays light around it.
4. Say only what is true: no invented clients, metrics or claims.
