## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio's site is a Slovak school primer (šlabikár): each product is a primer page, its initial letter at monumental scale, its picture, its word split into syllables. Refuses the dark-glow "tech studio" hero with gradient text and icon-tile service grids.

OWN-WORLD: Flat offset colour plates on white stock: yolk #f6c21c, cobalt #1f4fbf, grass #2f9a4c, vermilion #e8462a, ink #141414. No borders, boxes, shadows or gradients; grouping by colour field only. Gabarito 800–900 for letters and heads, Atkinson Hyperlegible for prose. Handwriting guide lines (linajky) as the baseline grid and the only ruled line.

STORY: A visitor sees four apps at once, knows each in one word, taps one; lower down they learn the same people build software for clients and email them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (wordmark left, Apps / Studio / Contact right). One-line hook at ~72px. Below, four full-height plates A·A·K·P filling the width, each with its letter at ~280px, the phone screen, the syllabified name and a tag; whole plate is the link.

FORM: Šlabikár primer, candidate 6 of 7. Raises: no boxes (cathode), numbered margin column (orizuru), one ruled grid (scope), prose as calm clearing (secession). seed:2c86296a2b4605f0
